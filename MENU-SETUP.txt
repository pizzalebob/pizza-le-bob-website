Pizza le Bob — Menu & Photos editor update

Install in the existing pizzalebob/pizza-le-bob-website GitHub repository.
1. Download and extract this ZIP.
2. In the repository, use Add file > Upload files.
3. Drag index.html and the admin, functions and assets folders from the extracted ZIP into the upload area. Keep their folder paths. Upload at the repository root, not inside another folder.
4. Commit the upload. Cloudflare will deploy the update automatically.
5. After deployment succeeds, refresh your editor and choose Menu & Photos.

Existing Cloudflare CONTENT storage and Access settings are reused. No new keys or bindings are required. Existing enquiries and pop-up dates continue to use their current setup.

Edit names, descriptions and prices. Add or remove items, hide items, and move them up or down. Upload JPG, PNG or WebP photos (up to 20 MB); the editor resizes them to a maximum of 1200 pixels and compresses them before saving. Export HEIC photos as JPG first. Up to 40 menu items are supported.

Weekly special details remain in the Pop-ups & special tab. Its photo can be changed in Menu & Photos. Save to website publishes both tabs together. Photos are public once saved; use pictures you have permission to publish. Cloudflare may take a short time to show saved changes in every location.

Verification: inline JavaScript syntax, authenticated content saves, rejection of unauthenticated writes and invalid photo sources, preservation of the menu when older clients save dates, editor tab/edit/add/hide/save interactions, public rendering, pizza selection and empty menu behaviour. Browser visual testing and a live authenticated save were not available in this session.
