These figures count browsers that allow anonymous visitor analytics. A unique visitor is counted once for the selected week or cumulative period, even when they view several pages. Overall unique visitors are deduplicated across all pages and weeks. Returning browsers count again in each weekly figure. Different devices, clearing browser data or withdrawing and allowing analytics again can count the same person again. Visitors who decline analytics, use privacy signals, block scripts or cannot send events are excluded. Visits to the editor are excluded.
